# Cookie Policy

*Last updated: November 5, 2026*

## 1. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website, typically used to remember preferences or analyze traffic.

## 2. Cookies Used on This Website

The **Shakespeare Central Apartment** website does not use cookies for advertising, profiling, or tracking users' activity.

The website uses **Vercel Web Analytics** to measure traffic in aggregate (pages visited, referring website, country, device type, and browser). This service does not use cookies and does not allow us to identify visitors. The website does not use other analytics or tracking services such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel.

Otherwise, only technologies or technical data that are strictly necessary for the operation and security of the website may be used, where required.

If this changes in the future (for example, if analytics tools that use cookies are introduced), we will update this policy and, if required, request your consent through a cookie banner.

## 3. Third-party cookies

The Site contains links to third-party platforms: **Booking.com** (for bookings), **Google Maps** (for locating attractions), and **Facebook** and **Instagram** (social media). If you follow these links, you will be taken to those platforms, which may set their own cookies according to their own privacy/cookie policies, over which we have no control.

The Contact page offers an interactive **Google Maps** map. The map is not loaded automatically: it only loads after you click the "Show map" button. Once loaded, Google may set its own cookies and process data such as your IP address, according to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The website's fonts are hosted on our own server, so no data is sent to third parties (such as Google Fonts) when loading them.

## 4. How to manage cookies

You can manage, block, or delete cookies directly from your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.). To clear the locally stored language preference, you can clear this Site's local storage (`localStorage`) from your browser settings.

Note that fully blocking cookies/local storage may affect how some websites function (including remembering your language preference on this Site).

## 5. Updates to this policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated if the technologies or services used on the website change.

The current version will be available on this page and will include the date of the latest update.
